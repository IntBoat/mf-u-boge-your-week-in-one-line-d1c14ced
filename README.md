A make.fun one-line entry: a single line of at most 100 characters that sums
up the week, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`, where the
competition's sealed scorer reads it. An AI judge then compares entries two
at a time and keeps the sharpest line.

Competition: https://make.fun/api/competitions/u-boge-your-week-in-one-line-d1c14ced/requirements
